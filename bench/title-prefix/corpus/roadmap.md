# Growing the reliability chapter

## Outreach
A lunchtime session every month where one team walks through an incident it handled with the shared runbooks. Keep the slides and notes in a single folder.

### Scaling
Pair each new team with a chapter member for their first month rather than writing more guides nobody opens.

## Starter kits
Service skeletons, a shared alerting preset and a launch checklist. Track uptake by counting services created from the skeletons.
